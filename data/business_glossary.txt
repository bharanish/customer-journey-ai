Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):
The average cost of acquiring a new customer.
Formula: Total Marketing and Sales Spend / Number of New Customers.

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):
The total revenue a business expects to earn from a customer over the entire relationship.

Conversion Rate:
The percentage of users who complete a desired action.
Formula: Conversions / Total Visitors × 100.

Customer Churn:
The percentage of customers who stop using a product or service during a specific period.

Retention Rate:
The percentage of customers retained over a given period.
Formula: Retained Customers / Total Customers × 100.

Return On Ad Spend (ROAS):
Measures revenue generated for every dollar spent on advertising.
Formula: Revenue from Ads / Advertising Spend.

Return On Investment (ROI):
Measures profitability relative to investment cost.
Formula: (Gain from Investment - Cost of Investment) / Cost of Investment × 100.

Revenue:
The total income generated from business operations before expenses.

Gross Revenue:
Total revenue generated before deductions such as refunds, discounts, and taxes.

Net Revenue:
Revenue remaining after deductions such as refunds, discounts, and allowances.

Average Order Value (AOV):
The average amount spent per order.
Formula: Total Revenue / Number of Orders.

Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):
The predictable monthly revenue generated from subscription customers.

Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):
The predictable yearly revenue generated from subscription customers.

Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT):
A metric that measures customer satisfaction based on survey responses.

Net Promoter Score (NPS):
Measures customer loyalty by asking how likely customers are to recommend a product or service.

Click Through Rate (CTR):
The percentage of users who click on an advertisement or link.
Formula: Clicks / Impressions × 100.

Cost Per Click (CPC):
The amount paid for each click on an advertisement.
Formula: Advertising Spend / Total Clicks.

Cost Per Lead (CPL):
The cost required to generate a lead.
Formula: Marketing Spend / Number of Leads.

Bounce Rate:
The percentage of visitors who leave a website after viewing only one page.

Lead:
A person or organization that has shown interest in a company's product or service.

Qualified Lead:
A lead that has met predefined criteria indicating a higher likelihood of becoming a customer.

Sales Funnel:
The journey a prospect takes from awareness to becoming a customer.

Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL):
A lead identified by the marketing team as more likely to become a customer.

Sales Qualified Lead (SQL):
A lead vetted by the sales team and considered ready for direct sales engagement.

Engagement Rate:
A measure of interactions such as likes, comments, clicks, or shares relative to audience size.

Impressions:
The number of times content, advertisements, or pages are displayed to users.

Customer Journey:
The complete sequence of interactions a customer has with a company before and after purchase.

Attribution:
The process of assigning credit to marketing channels or touchpoints that influence conversions.

Funnel Conversion Rate:
The percentage of users who move from one funnel stage to the next.

Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):
The average revenue generated per customer or user.
Formula: Total Revenue / Number of Users.

Lifetime Revenue:
The total revenue generated by a customer during the entire business relationship.

Cross-Sell:
The practice of encouraging customers to purchase related products or services.

Upsell:
The practice of encouraging customers to purchase a higher-value product or service.

Market Share:
The percentage of total market sales captured by a company.

Revenue Growth Rate:
The percentage increase in revenue over a specific period.
Formula: (Current Revenue - Previous Revenue) / Previous Revenue × 100.
